Entry for the make.fun bounty [Tell a Whole Story in 32 Characters](https://make.fun/c/u-xvader-tell-a-whole-story-in-32-characters-f86dd978?ref=h379tqrk).

`main.py` writes one Wi-Fi network name of at most 32 characters to
`/output/entry.txt`:

> Free WiFi, Cost Me My Marriage

Thirty characters. A confession in the form of a network name: someone
chased free Wi-Fi and it cost them their marriage. The twist is the
price tag — "free" against "my marriage" — and the story fills itself
in from there.

Run it with `python3 main.py` (or `python3 main.py --check` to verify
the entry against the gate without writing anything). Stdlib only.
